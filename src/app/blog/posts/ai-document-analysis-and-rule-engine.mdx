## The Evolution of Healthcare Claims Processing

Managing healthcare or national insurance claims in Indonesia involves an incredibly complex process of matching documents (eligibility letters, medical resumes, receipts). Historically, this process has relied heavily on manual labor. Today, however, we can leverage the combined automation of **AI-powered Document Analysis** and a code-based **Rule Engine**.

The combination of these two technologies is the primary foundation for building a Casemix system that is not only exceptionally fast but also highly precise.

## Phase 1: AI-Powered Document Extraction

Before a rule engine can function, unstructured data contained within PDF documents or scanned files must be transformed into neatly structured data (such as JSON format). This is where AI plays a vital role.

Using cutting-edge document analysis capabilities from Google Gemini, the system can read complex medical record structures—ranging from chief complaints and diagnoses to surgical procedures. The AI is configured through specific *prompting* to extract:
- **Patient Demographic Data**: Name, Card Number, Date of Birth
- **Primary Clinical Data**: Primary Diagnosis (ICD-10), Secondary Diagnoses, and Procedures (ICD-9-CM)
- **Chronological Data**: Admission Date and Discharge Date

The advantage of modern *vision-language models* is their ability to tolerate variations in form formats across different healthcare facilities.

## Phase 2: Centralized Validation with a Rule Engine

Once the data is successfully extracted, the next step is validation. This data is passed into a **Rule Engine** that instantly processes hundreds of business logic rules.

Some examples of automated validations performed:
1. **Demographic Consistency**: Ensuring alignment between the patient's gender and the medical procedure (e.g., pregnancy-related procedures cannot be assigned to male patients).
2. **Diagnosis and Procedure Alignment**: Validating whether the ICD-10 diagnosis code is clinically aligned with the claimed ICD-9-CM procedure code.
3. **Eligibility Verification**: Matching the SEP (Participant Eligibility Letter) number with uploaded eligibility Excel data to ensure active participation.

By utilizing Go (Golang) and the Fiber framework, these validations are executed on a massive scale concurrently (*batch processing*), allowing hospitals to process thousands of claims without bottlenecks.

## Towards Fraud Detection and Predictive Analytics

The strong synergy between AI and the rule engine opens up various advanced analytical opportunities:
- **Fraud Detection:** Patterns of claim manipulation can be quickly detected and annulled. This ranges from *upcoding* (submitting more expensive diagnoses without strong medical basis) and *phantom billing* (fictitious patient claims) to *unbundling*.
- **Fragmentation Analysis:** Analyzing treatment histories to detect the splitting of medical procedures (which should be claimed as a single package) in order to manipulate claim limits.
- **Readmission Prediction:** AI can analyze a patient's condition upon discharge and predict the probability of readmission within the near future.

With a solid hybrid validation system (AI and Logic Rules) as its foundation, the future of healthcare insurance management can be fully automated, minimizing claim disputes and preventing losses due to fraud.
